## Lean Full-Lifecycle convergence addendum

In addition to spec/code convergence, assess Definition of Output Done evidence:

- Acceptance Criteria satisfied;
- required CI/tests/security checks green;
- migration/compatibility/rollback concerns resolved;
- required operability and observability present;
- spec impact reconciled;
- no blocking Constitution/risk-policy violation;
- brownfield quality ratchet respected;
- exceptions explicit, narrow, owned, and reviewable.

Do not mark outcome value as validated during convergence. Outcome validation is
asynchronous and requires an outcome record plus product/analytics authority.
> Apply the canonical installed policy under `.specify/presets/lean-full-lifecycle-governance/policy/` when present.
