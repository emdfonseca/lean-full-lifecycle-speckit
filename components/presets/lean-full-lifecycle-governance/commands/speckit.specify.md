## Lean Full-Lifecycle specification addendum

Keep the specification focused on intended observable behavior.

Ensure it distinguishes:

- item-specific Acceptance Criteria;
- optional Outcome Criteria only when this item has an independently measurable
  value hypothesis;
- material product/security/privacy/data/performance/accessibility constraints;
- explicit out-of-scope boundaries;
- risk classification inputs;
- spec impact and authoritative-source references.

Do not copy tracker status, owners, sprint dates, implementation tasks, or
generic Engineering Excellence rules into the specification.

For brownfield work, current code/tests are evidence—not automatically intended
behavior. Record unresolved intent explicitly rather than converting inferred
behavior into requirements.
> Apply the canonical installed policy under `.specify/presets/lean-full-lifecycle-governance/policy/` when present.
