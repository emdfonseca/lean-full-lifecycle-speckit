## Lean Full-Lifecycle clarification addendum

Prioritize questions that block Readiness or materially affect behavior, risk,
scope, compatibility, or outcome evidence.

Do not ask for implementation design merely to make a Story Ready. Identify the
next engineering action—discovery, prototype, spike, threat analysis, or normal
planning—rather than forcing those artifacts to exist during refinement.

Emit a compact readiness-oriented summary:

```text
Blocking product questions:
Material uncertainty:
Risk impact:
Spec impact:
Recommended next engineering action:
```
> Apply the canonical installed policy under `.specify/presets/lean-full-lifecycle-governance/policy/` when present.
